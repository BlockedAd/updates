## What it does NOT do

We prefer to say this up front:

- It does **not** block ads inside YouTube, Facebook or Instagram. Those ads come from the same servers as the videos and posts, so no blocker of this kind can remove them.
- No blocker can guarantee that every single ad is blocked.
- Android allows only one VPN at a time, so BlockedAd can't run together with another VPN app.

## Privacy

- Everything happens on your phone. No account, no servers of ours, no data collected.
- BlockedAd uses Android's **local VPN** to check which addresses apps ask for and, if you block an app, to stop its connections. All of this happens on the phone itself: your traffic is not routed through any server and your IP address doesn't change.
- Once a day the app downloads updated blocklists and checks this page for a new version. Nothing about you is sent.

## How to install

1. Open the [download page](https://blockedad.gumroad.com/l/app?ref=github). Enter **0** as the price and tap the button below the price. Then enter only your email and tap **Get**. You do **not** need to enter a card number – the basic version is free.
2. Download the file **blockedad.apk** and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.
3. If Google Play Protect suggests a scan, tap **Scan app**, then **Install**.
4. Open BlockedAd and tap the big button. Android asks once to allow the VPN – tap **OK**.

The app updates itself: it tells you when a new version is ready.

## Why is BlockedAd not on Google Play?

Google Play does not allow apps that block ads in other apps. That is why ad blockers of this kind are offered outside Google Play.

Because the app is not from Google Play, Android shows a **Play Protect** message during installation. This is normal for every app installed from outside Google Play. Tap **Scan app** and then **Install**.
